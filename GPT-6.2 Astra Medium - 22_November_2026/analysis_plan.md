# Analysis plan | frozen before fitting
The requested documented binary epidemic endpoint is absent. No confirmatory model can meet that constraint. Independently define a supplementary prediction target: probability that an untreated research plot has S ≥ 10 percent, a round, interpretable severity threshold. This is a protocol assumption, not a threshold taken from the deposit or selected by model fit. Check the strict inequality separately. No treatment decision threshold or cost model is assumed.

Issue at 00 UTC on recorded flowering date. Require YAML pre period and strictly negative end day; precomputed UTC summaries only. The metadata disagree on inclusive endpoint conventions. Avoid day zero even for nominal PRE variables. Assume station data have arrived by issuance; actual reporting latency and real-time recognition of anthesis are unavailable.

Compare an intercept-only training prevalence (reference), a single linear predictor counting hours with VPD ≤0.635 kPa at days −15 to −5 (preferred weather specification), and that predictor plus mean temperature at days −20 to −10. Low VPD duration represents opportunity for sustained moist conditions in the approach to flowering. Temperature may add information about the preceding thermal environment. These are hypotheses about proxies, not mechanistic or causal effects. Scale duration per 24 qualifying hours and temperature per 5 °C, centered at 15 °C. No outcome screening, interactions, splines, cut point search, tuning or imputation. With complete candidate summaries compare like rows. If missing, restrict all specifications to their common complete cases and quantify exclusions. Keep the one-slope model as the parsimonious reference; consider the extra slope only if it improves both grouped Brier scores and does not worsen calibration materially. No claim of deployment readiness from this comparison.

Audit eligible-feature Spearman correlations, duplication, class balance across groups and predictor support. Never enter two competing moisture summaries together. Check sensitivity by replacing duration with number of days whose mean RH exceeds 70% at days −40 to −10 (changes metric and window); additionally shift only the temperature window to −25 to −15. Contrast the broader VPD threshold ≤1.1 kPa in the same window. Retain all results irrespective of direction.

Leave out entire years (primary, new season) and entire states (geographic transport proxy). Neither identifies a station or a field. Also test forward in time, training through 2008 and testing 2009–2015 as a single untouched chronological block for the prespecified specifications. Use fixed raw transforms and refit intercept/slopes within training partitions. Score plot-weighted pooled out-of-fold probabilities with ROC AUC, Brier score and calibration-in-the-large (offset logit) and joint calibration slope; draw binned reliability curves. Report Brier and AUC by fold, use undefined AUC for one-class groups, distinguish pooled from within-group discrimination. Plot outcomes receive equal weight in the principal analysis. Reweight equal state/date clusters for a sensitivity fit and score. Use year-cluster sandwich uncertainty for full-sample coefficients and inspect leave-year-out coefficient ranges and influential rows. Treat all uncertainty as conditional on these sampled environments. Perform the literature comparison only after documenting results and assessment.
