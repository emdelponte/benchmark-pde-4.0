# Data audit | before model fitting
Zenodo 23189372, version 2.1.0. All nine deposited files were downloaded with published MD5 checks. The release documents 999 unique row IDs for fungicide-untreated US small-plot observations, not 999 independent epidemics. YAML and Arrow agree on all 337 field names and their order. The eight nonweather fields are row ID, year, state, UTC flowering date, wheat type, cultivar resistance class, corn residue and severity. Factor fields retain dictionary encoding; corn is an integer indicator with nulls. Numeric empirical ranges and rounded missing percentages agree with the dictionary, documented aliases match their partners and no values violate supplied numeric validity bounds. See outputs/schema.csv for the column-by-column audit.

**Outcome discrepancy.** The sole documented response is S, FHB index (percent spike symptoms), range 0–86.18, median 2.87, IQR 0.20–11.31. Neither YAML, README, record description nor codebook supplies an epidemic indicator or a cutoff. Corn is binary but agronomic. Event counts for the requested documented endpoint are unidentified. For the separately defined S ≥ 10% target there are 273 events and 726 nonevents (27.33%); exactly two values equal 10.

**Coverage and dependence.** There are 32 observed years from 1982–2015 (1983 and 1985 absent) and 17 states. Annual sizes range from 1 to 167; 2009 contributes 167 plots, while each of the first six observed years contributes one. State sizes range from 4 in Arkansas to 265 in North Dakota. Nine years and four states have one outcome class. Full counts and prevalence appear in outputs/audit_year.csv and audit_state.csv. Of 492 state-by-flowering-date groups, 260 have repeated observations (maximum ten plots), and 63 contain both outcomes. There are 545 distinct full weather profiles and two duplicate rows after dropping ID. Different plots can have identical records; retain them and test weighting sensitivity. Clusters may include unrelated sites within a state, or split a station across flowering dates. No site, coordinates, station, cultivar identity or experiment ID permits reconstruction of the sampling hierarchy. The number of independent units is unknown. Neither the 999 rows nor the 492 proxy clusters give a defensible scalar effective sample size.

**Weather and timing.** The 329 weather fields comprise 174 pre, 137 post and 18 spanning anthesis. Temperature, dewpoint and dewpoint depression are in °C, RH in percent, pressure in hPa, VPD in kPa, counts in days or hours, and one product in hours squared. Daily and hourly summaries include fixed UTC full-day and overnight intervals. Nearest available reporting stations include citizen stations. All supplied weather summaries and outcomes are complete. Only corn has missing cells (4). Hourly source series, contributing-hour counts, station distance, gap handling and real-time latency cannot be audited from complete summaries alone.

The README sets anthesis at 00 UTC. YAML defines closed integer-day windows, while all 329 legacy window lengths differ by one day from the inclusive YAML lengths. Thus a window labeled PRE ending at 0 cannot be guaranteed available at issuance. Only 23 weather columns explicitly end before zero. Exclude the other 306. Absolute dates, severity, row ID and agronomic grouping variables are excluded as features. The README contents list omits the YAML and Parquet despite describing their addition.

**Predictor support.** Among 253 strictly pre-anthesis predictor pairs, four have absolute Spearman correlation ≥0.90. The two candidate VPD thresholds correlate at 0.935. Low-VPD hours and earlier mean temperature correlate at −0.269. The former span 46–238 hours (IQR 127–173), the latter 9.76–24.49 °C (IQR 14.57–18.24). Extremes fall within documented ranges; retain them and inspect influence after fitting. A one- or two-slope analysis is feasible given the observed events, but fitting 23 terms, much less searching over 329, would spend information needed for transport assessment. Historical variable curation also limits the independence of any internal validation.
