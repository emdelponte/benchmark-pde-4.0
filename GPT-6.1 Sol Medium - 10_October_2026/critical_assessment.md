# Final critical assessment — literature-informed continuation

The data permit a small exploratory logistic model once the later literature search supplies the binary S>=10% definition. The initial inability to identify the target from the release remains a valid metadata finding. Predictors were specified independently before the literature search; the fitted analysis itself was not wholly independent. This distinction is preserved in the original plan, revision, log and snapshot.

The provisional two-term model improves year-out Brier from 0.1995 (moisture alone) to 0.1932 and AUC from 0.541 to 0.616. The quadratic third parameter adds no benefit; rejecting it supports parsimony. However, the paired year-block Brier interval crosses zero, discrimination uncertainty is broad (AUC 0.528–0.713), and performance varies across years/states. Selection of the provisional specification using these validation results creates residual optimism; no second external dataset has evaluated the resulting choice. The held-out historical predictions should not be described as prospective validation.

Calibration is the strongest reason to withhold operational use. Pooled year-out calibration-in-large is near zero while the slope is 0.773; state-out slope falls to 0.610. The temporal holdout overpredicts mean risk (31.32% versus 26.08%). Resistance-class frequencies are far more discrepant: VS observed 46.67% versus predicted 26.63%, MR 12.97% versus 27.38%. A weather-only probability for this cultivar mixture is not a cultivar-specific risk. Leaving out resistance may also confound weather coefficients through geography, wheat type and era; positive weather associations cannot be assigned a causal interpretation.

Two predictor parameters are reasonable for a fixed exploratory fit with 273 event rows and 726 nonevent rows, and MLE fits converge. But only 171 distinct full-weather profiles carry events, with 53 profiles containing both event and nonevent rows. Dependence and host variation preclude an exact independent-event count. Year-clustered uncertainty uses 32 uneven years and ignores persistent site correlations across years; bootstrap intervals based on fixed cross-validated probabilities omit full refitting and upstream feature curation. Nominal sample size and confidence intervals do not certify model validity.

Year-deletion coefficient signs remain positive, and duplicate removal/profile balancing barely change performance. These are useful but narrow stability findings. Replacing the thermal window worsens validation; this means the reported risk relationship depends on the exposure definition, not that the selected interval is a proven biological mechanism. The limited safe moisture windows cannot assess robustness immediately before anthesis. Closed-window documentation conflicts with the CSV for every weather summary, and no raw timestamped series resolves it. The chosen windows strictly end before day 0 under either endpoint-count interpretation, but their exact biological duration remains uncertain.

Cook distance identifies no single dominant observation (maximum 0.0138 for the two-term model), yet row diagnostics cannot exclude influential environments or shared station artifacts. Extreme-value scans were kept descriptive. No cleaning rule was invented from disease outcomes, and no environment was silently removed.

The workflow's meaningful departure from common retrospective studies is a strict prediction-time screen and grouped/temporal checks. This does not prove published performance is optimistic by any particular amount: their targets, agronomic predictors and timing scopes differ. The low-dimensional model's modest accuracy could reflect missing immediate infection weather, omitted cultivar information, restricted candidate choice, exposure measurement error or true limited signal; this dataset alone cannot discriminate these explanations.

Next scientifically useful work would reconstruct raw weather windows ending before actual issuance time, resolve station/site and trial identities, obtain replicates/assessment protocols and resistance-specific target definitions, then preregister a limited weather-plus-host model and validate in later independent seasons/sites. Prospective data should represent the intended cultivar and management population and record station availability/QC at prediction time. Decision utility needs explicit costs of missed epidemics and unnecessary intervention; no probability action cutoff or toxin prediction is inferred here.

## Preserved independent critique

### Independent critical assessment

The strongest result is an audit finding, not a risk equation: the requested binary target is undocumented, and most nominally pre-anthesis predictors fail the strict prediction-time check. Column-name matching and complete released weather summaries could conceal both problems. Declining to fabricate coefficients or performance is necessary for scientific validity.

Strengths of this workflow include checksum-verified inputs, direct YAML interpretation, cross-format/schema checks, outcome-blind correlation assessment, explicit UTC availability rules, fixed low-dimensional candidate specifications, and validation planned around years and states. The original plan precedes fitting and literature lookup. No unsuccessful model is hidden: models were not run because the essential outcome gate failed.

The candidate moisture/temperature summaries are biologically plausible proxies for conditions supporting inoculum development, but they miss the immediate flowering infection environment, actual rain/wetness, residue inoculum and cultivar-specific susceptibility. Pressure is not rainfall. Association would not identify causation, and weather-only risks depend on the sampled host/management mixture. Validating a moisture proxy does not validate the exact 0.635-kPa threshold mechanistically.

999 rows overstate independent exposure information. Even 545 distinct weather profiles are not necessarily independent: seasons, station catchments, overlapping windows and shared trials remain. There is no event-per-parameter calculation for an undefined target. If a binary definition is established, one-to-three predictor parameters could be defensible relative to row counts, but environmental event diversity, separation, shrinkage needs and calibration variability must decide adequacy. A mechanical events-per-parameter rule is inadequate.

The planned leave-one-year-out validation avoids splitting repeated environments within years, but uses future years to predict earlier years; it does not reproduce prospective deployment. Leave-one-state-out checks regional transfer only, not truly unseen trial sites. Tiny/single-class groups can make AUC and calibration unavailable. Calendar changes in resistance composition and observation intensity may undermine pooled metrics. Bootstrap uncertainty based only on saved out-of-fold predictions would omit model-refitting/feature-selection uncertainty, and year-clustered coefficient inference would have only 32 uneven groups.

Strong predictor redundancy and ambiguous day inclusion make exhaustive search particularly risky. Fixed candidates prevent a new data-driven selection search, but this released set was already curated using prior studies (README says uninformative variables were removed). Thus even an independent analysis of the released columns cannot guarantee independence from earlier outcome-informed feature engineering. True raw-data reconstruction and external prospective validation are needed.

No environmental outlier was discarded; 115 rows exceed a descriptive z-score screening bound across 23 variables. This multiple-variable screening does not prove measurement error or model influence. The two duplicate row pairs could be genuine replicates or data duplication. Removing them without provenance would impose an unsupported assumption.

Unresolved needs: a documented epidemic cutoff/operator and target level, disease assessment/replicate protocol, actual site/trial/station identifiers and coordinates, station distances and QC, raw timestamped weather with missing-hour rules, local-time conversion and aggregation code, inoculum/management descriptors, prospective sampling and a clear decision context. A prospective cohort should lock a model and threshold before outcome collection, use only weather truly available at issuance, include new seasons/sites and relevant resistance classes, and evaluate probability calibration plus decision costs/benefits.

The independent analysis cannot say the sample is too small for every reasonable model or that prediction will fail. It can say the specified target is not identified by the release. Literature-based repair, if attempted, must be reported as a later change, not an independent original decision.
